# Design Brief: UI Mockup

## Brand Concept
* **Name:** UI (Universal Workspace)
* **Tagline:** Everything app for your engineering teams.
* **Vibe:** Highly technical, futuristic, dark, and energetic. Inspired by [Huly.io](https://huly.io/).
* **Color Palette:**
  * `--bg-primary`: `#090a0c` (deep space charcoal/black)
  * `--bg-secondary`: `#121316` (raised dark gray cards)
  * `--accent-blue`: `#3d7eff` (electric azure)
  * `--accent-purple`: `#903eff` (violet glow)
  * `--text-primary`: `#ffffff`
  * `--text-secondary`: `#8a8f98`
* **Typography:**
  * Headings: `Space Grotesk`, sans-serif (bold, geometric, slightly futuristic)
  * Body: `Inter`, sans-serif (highly readable, clean)

## Key Layout Sections
1. **Header (Nav):** Translucent glassmorphic fixed bar with logo, resources list, and a glowing "Sign In" button.
2. **Hero Section:** Large title displaying *"Everything App for engineering teams"* with a subhead, followed by a double-border-beam CTA button that glows. Below the copy, a dashboard mockup sits masked behind a cursor-tracking circular "flashlight" reveal.
3. **Features Bento Grid:**
   * Card 1: Team Planner (with timeline illustration).
   * Card 2: Notifications (with live beacon glows).
   * Card 3: Keyboard Shortcuts (with active border-beam highlight).
   * Card 4: Time-blocking (with responsive grid alignment).
4. **Interactive Virtual Office Conferencing Room:** A simulated call room showing participant video tiles, active speak indicator glows, and call controls.
5. **Footer:** Simple links, star count, and copyright markers.
